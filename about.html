Our approach

Well done, good and faithful servant.

Our name comes from the Parable of the Talents. We believe the money, skills, and opportunities you hold are entrusted to you, and that they deserve a plan.

The business concept

Good & Faithful Wealth Management is a planned registered investment advisory firm for faith-driven households. Most families know what they want: a home, college for the kids, a wedding, a secure retirement. Few have a clear, tax-aware plan to get there.

We build that plan around each household's goals, with a tiered fee priced slightly below the market average and no account minimum.

We don't promise to beat the market or pick winning stocks. Our value comes from planning discipline, tax efficiency, and investments that match your convictions.

Why it exists

The founder has managed his own money and his family's finances for years, and friends kept coming to him for advice. That led him to pursue the credentials to do it professionally.

The goal: make thoughtful, values-aligned financial planning available to more families, not only the very wealthy.

How every plan works

  1. Save 15% of income. Counting your own dollars only. Any employer match is a bonus.
  2. Capture the full employer match. It's an immediate return you can't get anywhere else.
  3. Max the Roth IRA. Using a backdoor Roth when income is too high for a direct contribution.
  4. Return to the pre-tax 401(k). Fill it to the annual limit.
  5. Invest the rest in a taxable brokerage. Flexible money for near-term goals.
